# Oil Supply Risk Dashboard
<img width="2485" height="1234" alt="app_img" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd06bba4-1163-470d-bf1f-74cf2833b9f1" />

A Python Streamlit MVP dashboard for visualizing major oil supply routes, chokepoints, market prices and scenario-based gasoline price impact estimates.

## Features

- Interactive world map of oil supply routes
- Key chokepoints:
  - Strait of Hormuz
  - Suez Canal
  - Bab el-Mandeb
  - Strait of Malacca
  - Turkish Straits
  - Panama Canal
  - Cape of Good Hope
- Live commodity market data:
  - Brent crude
  - WTI crude
  - RBOB gasoline
  - Heating oil
- Selected stock market data:
  - ExxonMobil
  - Chevron
  - Shell
  - BP
  - TotalEnergies
  - Equinor
  - Airline ETF
- Scenario simulator for disruptions
- Estimated crude and gasoline price pressure
